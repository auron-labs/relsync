and the scoring that chooses between them follow the `Categorizer` in the
ReleaseSync project, which carries this licence. The code was written anew in
Go. Duplicate keywords were dropped, the categories are stored under keys
that do not change when their names are translated, and the `ai-` name fragment
is matched with its hyphen at the start of a word, where the original dropped
the hyphen and matched any name containing "ai".
